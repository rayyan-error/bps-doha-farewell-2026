Evening Itinerary & Highlights

Five unforgettable chapters of elegance, tribute, music, and nostalgia under the West Bay skyline.

05:30 PM – 06:30 PM AST Chapter I • Foyer

Red Carpet Arrival & Mocktails

Graduating seniors from Grade 12 N and fellow sections make their grand entrance along the illuminated palm promenade at Hilton Doha. Welcomed with signature saffron-rose & mint citrus mocktails, boutonnieres, and live paparrazi portraits at the golden arch photo-op wall.

360° Glambot Booth Signature Mocktail Bar Batch Crest Pins
01

“Suits pressed, sarees draped, and fourteen years of schoolyard laughter converging onto one red carpet.”

06:30 PM – 07:30 PM AST Chapter II • Main Stage

Principal & Valedictorian Keynotes

Ceremonial lighting of the lamp followed by inspiring addresses from the Principal, Senior Vice-Principals, and Head Boy & Head Girl. A moving reflection on resilience, academic excellence, and carrying the Birla Public School torch across global universities.

Candlelight Vigil Principal's Blessing Valedictory Address
02

“Passing the flame of knowledge from Abu Hamour classrooms to horizons around the world.”

07:30 PM – 08:45 PM AST Chapter III • Spotlight

Musical Performances & Class Awards

Unplugged acoustic medleys by the Senior School Band, classical-contemporary fusion choreography, and the much-awaited Class Superlatives & Sash Ceremony — crowning Mr. & Ms. Valediction 2026 alongside lighthearted batch honors.

Senior Band Medley Mr. & Ms. BPS 2026 Class Superlative Sashes
03

“Every chord sung together in the ballroom echoed a thousand recess bells.”

08:45 PM – 09:45 PM AST Chapter IV • Culinary

Grand Dinner Buffet at Hilton

A curated five-star banquet prepared by Hilton Doha’s executive culinary team, featuring Levantine mezze, royal Indian dum biryani & kebabs, continental carving stations, and a bespoke three-tier navy-and-gold "Class of 2026" celebratory cake cutting.

5-Star International Buffet Ceremonial Cake Cutting Faculty & Student Tables
04

“Breaking bread one last time as classmates, teachers, and lifelong friends.”

09:45 PM – 11:00 PM AST Chapter V • Finale

DJ & Nostalgia Video Reel

Lights dim for the premiere of the 15-minute archival documentary tracing the Batch of 2026 from Kindergarten to Senior Year, culminating in the Final Roll Call, an electrifying DJ dance floor, and a golden confetti shower across the ballroom.

2012–2026 Memory Reel Final Roll Call Gold Confetti Finale
05

“Not a goodbye — only a golden 'see you soon' under the Doha night sky.”
