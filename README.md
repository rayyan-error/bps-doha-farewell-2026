# Valediction 2026 | Birla Public School, Doha, Qatar

> *"A Nostalgic Evening Under the Arabian Stars"* — Senior Batch Graduation & Farewell Gala (Class of 2026)

An interactive, mobile-first gala event showcase developed for **Birla Public School, Doha's Batch of 2026** graduation and farewell evening at the **Grand Ballroom, Hilton Doha, West Bay**.

## Highlights & Features

- **Hero & Commemorative Showcase**: School branding badge, dramatic typography, commemorative completion badge (*February 2026*), and animated attendance/batch counters (`450+ Attendees`, `Grade 12 N & Batches`, `1 Incredible Evening`).
- **Interactive Evening Itinerary**: Filterable and richly styled timeline covering Red Carpet Arrival & Mocktails, Principal & Valedictorian Keynotes, Musical Performances & Class Awards, Grand Dinner Buffet at Hilton, and the DJ & Nostalgia Video Reel.
- **Interactive Memories & Gallery Grid**: Curated visual photography layout, filterable memory cards, student batch tags, and modal lightbox preview with heartfelt quotes from the Class of 2026.
- **Venue & Commemoration Card**: Detailed showcase of the Grand Ballroom at Hilton Doha, Diplomatic Area, West Bay, with interactive Google Maps directions and a congratulatory message from the Valediction 2026 Organizing Committee.
- **Celebratory Experience**: Custom gold & midnight-blue starlight canvas particles and interactive `canvas-confetti` gold bursts upon page load and user trigger.

## Tech Stack

- **HTML5** (Semantic markup & accessible structure)
- **Tailwind CSS** (Custom midnight navy `#060B19` & Arabian gold `#D4AF37` theme)
- **Vanilla JavaScript** (Interactive filtering, lightbox gallery, starfield canvas, counter animations)
- **Canvas Confetti** (`canvas-confetti` for celebratory gold bursts)
- **GitHub Pages** (Static hosting & deployment)
